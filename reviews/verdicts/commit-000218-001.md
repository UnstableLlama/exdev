## Reviewer finding (not in the entry)

**Export does not round-trip for a layer that sets the flag to false.** The entry states both halves: loading
skips the transpose, and `LinearFP16.get_tensors` always transposes on the way out. Together they mean a layer
constructed with `transposed_load = False` and saved through `get_tensors` (an unquantized layer written by
conversion or compilation) is stored in conventional orientation under the same key, and the same constructor
then reads it back without transposing. For a non-square layer the shape no longer matches the padding target;
for a square one it is silently the transpose. Nothing in the tree sets the flag yet, so nothing reaches this.
Source reading only.
