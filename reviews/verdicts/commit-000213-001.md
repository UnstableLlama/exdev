## Reviewer knowledge (from the tensor headers in the bundle; never to a teacher)

- All five checkpoints have 451 BF16 tensors: 36 layers with the names the Llama constructor expects, plus
  embeddings, final norm and head.
- **Attention biases.** Every configuration sets `attention_bias` true and every layer stores `q_proj.bias`,
  `k_proj.bias` and `v_proj.bias` (no `o_proj` bias). Nothing in the tree reads `attention_bias`, but `Linear`
  loads a `.bias` tensor whenever one is present, so the biases are picked up. This is the Qwen2 situation, not
  the plain Llama one.
- **Unused MTP tensors.** Each checkpoint stores 16 tensors under `model.mtp_layers.0` (its own attention with
  biases, MLP, five norms and an `input_proj` of 4096 × 8192). They are never read, which is the "no MTP yet" of
  the commit message.
