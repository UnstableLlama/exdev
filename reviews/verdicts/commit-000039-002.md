## What the reference evidence did and did not do

It did what the user's decision was for: the entry now rests on a real configuration and real tensor shapes instead of saying none was available, and it bounds its claim to what it read ("covers Nemotron-Super's configuration and first tensor shard only, not every shard or the other catalogued Nemotron variants").

It did not surface the head-count line, and the reason is worth recording plainly. The teacher read one of the four attached configurations. In that one, every active block has 64 heads in groups of 8, so the group size and the number of groups are both 8 and the shapes agree either way. The three configurations it did not read have group sizes of 16, 32 and 64 against 64 or 128 heads, where the two readings differ. I established that from the attached, cutoff-eligible bundle, not from later code.

So the draft's corroboration is true and is also the one case that cannot distinguish the two readings. That is not a false statement and not a reason to reject.

**Why not a third attempt.** Re-authoring this commit again, or adding guidance now, until the teacher finds a defect the reviewer already knows about would be steering by hindsight, which the boundary exists to prevent. The reference rule has been applied as decided; this is its honest result. The defect is recorded when the walk authors the fix at position 44, as the retrospective policy says.
