## Reviewer-only: constructor against the checkpoints (not for any teacher)

From the 71 headers in the bundle, for each of the seventeen models I built the tensor list the constructor requests
and compared names and shapes.

- **All seventeen**: no declared tensor is missing and no shape differs. `q_norm` and `k_norm` weights are `[128]` in
  every layer, which is what an `RMSNorm` over the last axis of a per-head tensor expects. So unlike Command R Plus,
  Qwen3's normalisation weights fit the hook added at commit 108.
- **Tied-embedding models** differ in storage: `Qwen3-0.6B`, `Qwen3-1.7B` and their FP8 variants store an `lm_head.weight`
  tensor although the config says tied, so the head loads its own tensor; `Qwen3-4B`, the tied Base variants and
  `Qwen3-4B-FP8` store none and take the embedding fallback. The constructor's "tied and no `lm_head` tensor" test
  handles both.
- **The six FP8 variants** store their projection weights as `F8_E4M3` with a `weight_scale_inv` tensor beside each
  (196 to 448 such tensors per model). The loader's dtype table at this commit has no entry for that type, so these
  repositories are a different storage format and cannot load through this constructor. The entry says FP8 was not
  validated; the headers show it is unsupported.
- The eleven ordinary checkpoints are BF16 throughout, converted to float16 by the loader. Every width is a multiple
  of 128.
