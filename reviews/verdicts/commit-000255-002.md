now imports `integration`, whose initialiser imports the adapter and, with it, the Transformers quantizer
APIs, "even when patch_transformers is never called". It then stops. It does not ask what that requires of
an environment or of existing callers of the package, and it does not compare the new import with what the
project declares. I had to make that check myself, and its outcome is the most consequential effect of this
commit on anyone not using the new feature. Under the standard in force, a central check left to the
reviewer is a rejection however it comes out. (The same family of question went unasked at 249; there it was
beside the commit's subject and I approved with a note. Here the entry itself identifies the mechanism as
Impact, so it is not peripheral.)
