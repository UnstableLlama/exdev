# Verdict: commit-000110-002 (replacement of 110/001; teacher Sol, guidance 11)

## Decision

**Not approved as a replacement. 110/001 stays the entry in force.** Nothing in 110/002 is false; it is
declined because it is not a superset of the entry it would replace.

## Why

110/001 states the commit's tied-head mechanism: `model.embed_tokens` becomes the head's alternate key only
when `tie_word_embeddings` is true and the collection holds no `lm_head`. 110/002 lists which configurations
tie and never mentions the mechanism or the head tensor. The request says so itself, which is to its credit.

This matters here because the reference checkpoints split on exactly that point. From the headers in the
packet (mine): `Qwen3-4B`, `Qwen3-4B-Base`, `Qwen3-0.6B-Base` and `Qwen3-1.7B-Base` store no `lm_head` and
depend on the fallback; `Qwen3-0.6B`, `Qwen3-1.7B` and all six FP8 variants store one although the smaller
ones declare tying; the 8B and larger models are untied and store one. An entry for this commit that reads
the headers and omits this is weaker on the head than the entry written without headers.

(Correction to my own 110/001 verdict: it listed `Qwen3-4B-FP8` among the repositories with no `lm_head`.
The header shows `lm_head.weight [151936, 2560]`. The mechanism and conclusion there are unaffected.)

## What 110/002 does well

- `check.py` clean, 63 of 63 calls replay, manifest differs from 110/001's in `guidance_version` only,
  provenance `gpt-6.1-sol` medium, request hashes match.
- Header statements all correct against the packet: Q/K norm weights `[128]`; Q `[128Q, H]`, K/V

- The observation that explicit `head_dim` matters (fallback division would give 64 or 80, not 128) is right.

the code requests are not checked against what the checkpoint holds, so absent tensors and the fallback paths
they select go unstated. Here it cost content the old entry had. I have asked the user whether to trial a
one-sentence addition to paragraph F; no trial is started without their word.
