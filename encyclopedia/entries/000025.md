## Problem

The parent time formatter rounds hour counts upward before subtracting whole hours, which can produce negative remaining minutes. Entry 000022 demonstrated the failure at 1,800 seconds.

## Reasoning

Uses floor division for total minutes and whole hours, then subtracts the whole-hour component. Adds an hours-only output when the minute remainder is zero. The exact diff was applied to the parent file and the extracted function was executed at 0, 59, 60, 1800, 3600 and 3660 seconds. Outputs were respectively < 1 minute, < 1 minute, 1 minute, 30 minutes, 1 hour and 1 hour, 1 minute.

## Impact

Repairs conversion ETA formatting without changing how remaining seconds are estimated. This is the final entry of the requested first-25 review batch.
